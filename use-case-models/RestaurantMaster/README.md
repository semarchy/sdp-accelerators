# Restaurant Master

This accelerator covers the **restaurant master data** of a quick-service restaurant group: one trusted record per
physical restaurant, across brands, countries and lifecycle stages, from pipeline site to closure. It brings
together the restaurant's public-facing data, its lifecycle, its operator and franchisee, its place in the
organization and the identifiers each system uses for it.

It is built for a fictional group, Copperline Restaurant Group, with three brands in the US and Canada. All names and
data in this accelerator are made up.

- **Entities:** 22 (Restaurant and Legal Entity are ID-matched, the others are basic)
- **Source systems:** Real Estate, ERP, three brand POS systems, plus data entry in the hub
- **Workflows:** onboard a restaurant, change restaurant data, change lifecycle status, transfer operator
- **Version:** built with the Semarchy DXP extension and Design CLI 2.2.0

Please refer to the [user guide](./documentation/user_guide.md) for detailed information.
